## 💡 Pro Tips

1. **Quantify Everything**: Use numbers ($2.2M, 9,800 records, 4 visualizations)
2. **Show Impact**: Focus on business value, not just technical skills
3. **Be Specific**: Mention exact tools (Pandas, not just "Python libraries")
4. **Tell a Story**: Problem → Solution → Results
5. **Keep it Concise**: 3-4 bullet points max per project
